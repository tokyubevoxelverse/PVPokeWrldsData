Get on the board

Your battles save in each world by themselves. To put them on the board:

1. Open Submit Your Statsat the leaderboard in any PV Pokémon World.
2. Copy the linkit makes for you (your name, wins and losses, signed).
3. Paste it and press SUBMITin the URL field below it.

Submitting needs Allow Untrusted URLs on in VRChat's settings. Seeing the board doesn't. Your display name and record become public.
